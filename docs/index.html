Predict expected sensor values from correlated channels. Flag large reconstruction mismatches for quality-control review and identify which sensor needs attention.

Open research

AI-ready data starts with trustworthy measurements.

Our broader research explores self-supervised quality control, graph-based sensor dependencies, and generative recovery of missing environmental observations.

What this version does

Applies the saved masked Transformer to ten familiar orchard channels, returns per-sensor residuals, and reconstructs eligible missing values using neighboring and cross-sensor context.

What comes next

Graph-enhanced representations, expert-reviewed quality labels, generalization across environments, and integration of separately trained diffusion-based gap reconstruction.
